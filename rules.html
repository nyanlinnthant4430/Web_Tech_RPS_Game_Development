How to Play

  • Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors to make your move
  • The computer will instantly pick its own move
  • ✊ Rock beats ✂️ Scissors
  • ✂️ Scissors beats 📄 Paper
  • 📄 Paper beats ✊ Rock
  • The result shows as Win, Lose, or Draw each round
  • Scores update automatically after every round
  • 🏆 Best of 3 — first player to 2 wins takes the match
  • 🏆 Best of 5 — first player to 3 wins takes the match
  • 🏆 Best of 7 — first player to 4 wins takes the match
  • Enter your name before playing to save your score to the Leaderboard
