How to Play

  • Choose Rock or Paper or Scissors
  • The computer will choose automatically
  • Rock beats Scissors
  • Scissors beats Paper
  • Paper beats Rock
  • The result will show win or lose or draw
  • The score updates after each round
  • Best of 3 modes need to 2 points wins
  • Best of 5 modes need first to 3 points wins
  • Best of 7 modes need first to 4 points wins
