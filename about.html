About This Project

  • Inspired by one of the most classic hand games in the world — a game we all played as kids to settle friendly arguments and share laughs.
  • A simple idea that carries excitement, strategy, and surprise in every round.
  • Created to bring the same joy into the digital world — fun, interactive, and available anytime, anywhere.
  • Whether you play to pass the time, challenge friends, or test your luck against the computer, every click brings a new chance to win.
  • Built with passion for creativity, simplicity, and fun — because great games don’t have to be complicated.
  • Sometimes the best experiences come from the simplest ideas: Rock, Paper, and Scissors.
